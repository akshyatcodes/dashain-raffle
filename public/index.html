      Event settings

      Ticket prices

      A single-ticket price is required. Add bundles like "3 for 500". Every sale is charged the cheapest combination.

      Self-service tickets

      Let staff reserve tickets from this site and pay a collector. Reservations stay out of the draw until finance confirms the payment, and unpaid ones lapse after the hold time.

      Payment collectors

      Who buyers pay: the finance team or a designated person. Each collector can have their own methods and a payment QR, which is shown to buyers when they pick them.
